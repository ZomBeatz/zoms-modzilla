# Changelog

## 0.1.0

**Downloads**
- Download mods from Nexus Mods **one at a time**, **several at once** (1 to 8), or **Match my account** (several at once with Premium, one at a time with a free account), switched on the Downloads page or in **Settings > Downloads**. The change takes effect right away: running files carry on, and the next ones follow the new setting.
- A **speed limit** for all downloads together, so games and calls on the same connection stay smooth.
- Checks there's room on the drive before a download starts, and shows the free space.
- Pause, resume, cancel and reorder files. A paused or interrupted download picks up where it stopped, even after closing the app.
- A dropped connection is tried again by itself, up to 3 times.
- Add files from a mod page address (Premium accounts), or with the **Mod manager download** button on nexusmods.com (every account).
- **Collections:** paste a collection's address, or use its button on nexusmods.com, and every file in it is queued, with a profile made from it.

**Browse**
- See Nexus Mods' trending, new and just-updated mods for each game, with pictures, and get their files in two clicks.

**Profiles and mod packs**
- A profile is a set of mods for one game, in order. **Play** puts its mods into the game's folder (Data for Skyrim and Fallout, Mods for Stardew Valley, and so on), takes the last profile's out, and starts the game. The game's own files are kept aside and put back.
- Bethesda games: the profile's plugins are switched on in the game's plugins.txt.
- **Check for conflicts** lists files more than one mod has (and which one wins), plugins that need a master that isn't there, and plugins loading before their master.
- **Save as mod pack** shares a profile as a small file listing the mods; **Open mod pack** makes the profile and queues what's missing.

**Library**
- **Check for updates** finds newer versions of the files you have, with a button to get each one, or all of them.
- Every mod is kept in its own folder under its game, with a `nexus.json` saying what it is.
- Zip, 7z and rar files can be unpacked when they finish, or later from the Library. Archives can be kept or removed after unpacking.

**Games**
- Cover art, banners and logos for every game: from Steam's own copy on your computer, Steam's image server, or Nexus Mods.
- Set where each game's mods go and how to start a game that isn't in Steam.
- Finds Nexus Mods games Steam has installed, native or through Proton, including Flatpak and Snap Steam and extra library drives. Add any other game by hand.

**Also**
- **Settings > Nexus Mods links** makes Zom's Nexus open nxm:// links.
- Light and dark themes that follow your computer.
